# Tether 0.3.2

Released 2026-10-24

## Highlights

- Option types your keyboard layout's characters again (⌥Q → @ on Turkish keyboards).
- No keychain question at every launch after an update.
- Claude Code's tables and boxes line up again in tabs restored after reopening Tether.

## Improved

- "Use Option as Meta key" is off by default, like Terminal.app: Option types the characters of your keyboard layout (@, [, ], {, }, |, \, ~ on many layouts). Settings saved by earlier versions are switched off once; turn it on in Settings ▸ Keyboard if you use Option as Meta in emacs or the shell.

## Fixed

- Tether no longer asks for access to "Tether security settings" in your keychain at every launch after an update. The copy is moved to a place Tether reads without a prompt (you may be asked one last time). Declining that prompt no longer shows a "Please check your security settings" warning.
- Tables, rules and input boxes of full-screen programs (Claude Code and others) no longer drift to the right in tabs restored after reopening Tether, or after moving a window between a Retina and a non-Retina display. Terminals now measure their cells again for the screen they are on.
