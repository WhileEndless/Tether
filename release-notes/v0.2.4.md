# Tether 0.2.4

Released 2026-10-23

## Highlights

- The sidebar follows the tab bar: the selected tab's session is highlighted under its host.
- Sessions under a host can be sorted: active first, by name, newest or oldest first, or in your own order by dragging.
- Tabs reorder live while you drag them: the other tabs slide out of the way, as in Safari.

## New

- Sort sessions under a host: Active First (default), Name, Newest First, Oldest First or Manual. Choose it in Settings ▸ General or from the right-click menu of a host or session. In Manual, drag sessions to reorder them; the order is kept after a relaunch.

## Improved

- Dragging a tab in the tab bar moves it as soon as it passes the middle of another tab, with the others sliding aside; the dragged tab stays dimmed in place. Holding it over the bar's arrows scrolls a long tab bar. Dragging to another window or out to a new window works as before.
- Selecting a tab selects its session (or file manager) in the sidebar and scrolls it into view, unfolding its host or group if needed, instead of leaving the last clicked sidebar row highlighted.

## Fixed

- Releasing a dragged tab over empty space in its own tab bar no longer sends it to the end.
